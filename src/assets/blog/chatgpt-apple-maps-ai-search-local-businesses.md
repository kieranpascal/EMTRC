## How London discovery quietly shifted from 10 blue links to 3 spoken recommendations

Two years ago, a South Kensington resident looking for a colour specialist or a Greenwich homeowner needing a Gas Safe landlord certificate typed three clipped keywords into Google and scanned ten links.

Today, an increasingly large slice of London customers — especially busy professionals on iPhones — type or speak full conversational prompts directly into **ChatGPT**, **Perplexity**, **Apple Intelligence / Siri** or **Google AI Overviews**:

- *"Recommend a reliable Gas Safe heating engineer in Greenwich or Blackheath who shows call-out prices upfront and has strong recent reviews."*
- *"What's the best independent sourdough café open before 8am near Peckham Rye with oat milk and vegan pastries?"*
- *"Find a Hackney barber open after 7pm on Thursdays where I can book a skin fade online."*

Notice the difference: an AI assistant does not hand back a scrolling directory of twenty listings. It shortlists **two or three specific businesses** and explains *why* it picked them. If your website hides prices inside a scanned JPEG, lacks structured data, or never mentions your exact postcodes and opening hours in plain text, the model simply skips you — even if your shop is forty yards away from the person asking.

> AI assistants do not "browse" your website the way a human admires a slideshow. They read clean HTML, `application/ld+json` structured data, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places and review text to verify facts with zero ambiguity.

## Where AI engines actually pull local London business facts

Most small-business owners assume Google Business Profile is the only directory that matters. That used to be true in 2019 — it is no longer true in 2026. Look at the exact upstream data sources feeding the four major discovery engines Londoners use every day:

| Discovery Engine | Primary Data Sources Checked | Biggest Blind Spot for London Shops |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ChatGPT Search** | Live web HTML, Bing Places, Schema.org JSON-LD, review aggregators | Relying solely on Instagram with no crawlable `.co.uk` site |
| **Apple Intelligence / Siri** | **Apple Business Connect**, website metadata, TripAdvisor/Yelp/Maps reviews | Unclaimed Apple Maps listing showing wrong 2021 hours |
| **Google AI Overviews** | Google Business Profile, website `LocalBusiness` schema, recent Google reviews | Missing upfront pricing and uncategorised Services list |
| **Perplexity** | Fast static HTML pages, clear Q&A text, local directories & press mentions | Slow JavaScript-heavy templates or PDF-only price lists |

In London — where iPhone market share among urban commuters sits well above 60% — leaving **Apple Business Connect** unclaimed is one of the easiest missed wins in local marketing. It costs £0, takes ten minutes to verify, and directly feeds Siri, Apple Maps and iOS Spotlight suggestions.

## The four technical pillars that make your business "AI-legible"

You do not need gimmicky "AI SEO software" subscriptions. Making a local London business effortlessly readable to AI search engines comes down to four durable engineering habits:

### 1. Valid, trade-specific `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD Schema

Every single emtrc website injects clean, validated `application/ld+json` structured data directly into the page `<head>` using the exact Schema.org subtype for your trade — not just generic `Organization`:

- `HairSalon` or `BarberShop` for barbers and stylists
- `Plumber`, `Electrician` or `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` for London trades
- `HousekeepingService` for local domestic and end-of-tenancy cleaners
- `CafeOrCoffeeShop` or `Restaurant` for neighbourhood hospitality

Inside that structured block, we declare your exact `name`, `telephone`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `priceRange`, `areaServed` (specific London boroughs and postcodes), `hasOfferCatalog` (your actual services and £ prices), and `sameAs` links tying together your website, Instagram and booking platform.

### 2. Plain-HTML prices, hours and borough service areas

If a customer asks ChatGPT *"How much is a skin fade at Kingsland Blade in Hackney?"*, the model looks for clean semantic text like `Skin Fade + Beard Trim — £36 (45 mins)`. When prices are trapped inside an Instagram Story Highlight or a flattened Canva image, retrieval systems cannot read them and default to recommending a competitor whose prices are published transparently in HTML.

### 3. Natural-language FAQ sections that mirror real customer prompts

Think about the five questions people ask on the phone every single week:

- *"Do you charge a call-out fee in SE10?"*
- *"Do you take walk-ins or appointments only?"*
- *"Are your cleaners insured and do they bring their own supplies?"*
- *"Are your pastries baked fresh daily and do you have gluten-free options?"*

Publishing crisp, honest 2–3 sentence answers to those exact questions on your website gives both anxious human buyers and AI answer engines direct, verifiable sentences they can quote verbatim.

### 4. Specific, detailed customer reviews across the last 90 days

Large language models weigh *descriptive* review phrases heavily. Compare two five-star reviews:

- Review A: *"Great service 5 stars!"*
- Review B: *"Arrived in Blackheath within 40 minutes when our Worcester Bosch boiler lost pressure on a Sunday morning. Fixed upfront for the quoted £95."*

Review B confirms your **borough**, **boiler brand**, **Sunday responsiveness** and **transparent pricing**. When someone asks an AI assistant for an honest Sunday boiler engineer near Blackheath, businesses backed by specific, recent reviews rise straight to the top.

## Your 20-minute AI visibility checklist for this week

1. **Test yourself right now**: Open ChatGPT and Perplexity and ask *"Who are the best [your trade] in [your London borough] with clear prices and good reviews?"* Note who gets named and inspect their websites.
2. **Claim Apple Business Connect** at `businessconnect.apple.com` and match your opening hours, photos and `.co.uk` link identically to Google Maps.
3. **Claim Bing Places** (`bingplaces.com`) — you can import directly from Google Business Profile in two minutes, immediately feeding Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT web retrieval.
4. **Replace PDF or image-only price lists** with crisp, fast-loading HTML service menus paired with valid `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD schema.

Getting cited by AI search is not magic — it is simply being the clearest, fastest, most trustworthy structured answer in your London postcode.
